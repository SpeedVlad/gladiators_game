## 🎮 Features

- **Dynamic Gladiator System**: Play as three distinct fighters with unique stat profiles and combat styles
- **Tactical Combat**: Turn-based battle mechanics with strategic depth
- **Character Progression**: Level up your gladiators and increase their combat effectiveness
- **Rich Statistics**: Constitution, Strength, Dexterity, Charisma, and more
- **Equipment System**: Shields and weapons to customize your fighter's loadout

## 🏆 The Gladiators

### Gallus - The Defender
Tank-focused warrior with high constitution and balanced defense.
- **Strength**: 2 | **Dexterity**: 1 | **Constitution**: 5 | **Charisma**: 3
- **HP**: 30 | **Basic Attack**: 10 | **Shield**: ✓

### Sagittarius - The Archer
Quick and precise, excels in ranged combat.
- **Strength**: 2 | **Dexterity**: 5 | **Constitution**: 2 | **Charisma**: 2
- **HP**: 20 | **Basic Attack**: 8 | **Shield**: ✗

### Spartacus - The Warrior
Raw power and combat mastery in a mobile frame.
- **Strength**: 5 | **Dexterity**: 2 | **Constitution**: 2 | **Charisma**: 1
- **HP**: 15 | **Basic Attack**: 14 | **Shield**: ✗

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Java 11 or higher
- Maven or Gradle

### Installation

```bash
git clone https://github.com/SpeedVlad/gladiators_game.git
cd gladiators_game
mvn clean install
```

### Running the Game

```bash
mvn exec:java -Dexec.mainClass="com.gladiators.Game"
```
